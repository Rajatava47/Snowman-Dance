How to Play ❄

❄ 1. Pick Opposite Roles: In the lobby, one player picks Play as Boy (Left) and the other picks Play as Girl (Right) in the same room.

🎵 2. 10-Second Rounds: The server starts each round with a 10-second countdown timer in the center.

💃 3. Match Complementary Moves:
  • Reach Out (Boy) + Take Hand (Girl)
  • Spin Her (Boy) + Twirl (Girl)
  • Dip (Boy) + Lean Back (Girl)

✨ 4. Score & Animate: Matching pairs increase Vibe by +20% and play the dance move animation! Mismatches decrease Vibe by -10%.

✉️ 5. Win & Share: Reach 100% Vibe to bring the couple together and exchange your romantic Winter Letter!
